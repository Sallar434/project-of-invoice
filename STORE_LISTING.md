## Full description

DueTrail is a follow-up workspace for freelancers who invoice clients directly. It is made by Sallar Hussain Bali.

The invoice is already sent from QuickBooks, Wave, or another tool. What gets lost is the rest: the accounts contact, the purchase order or portal step, the date the client promised, and the next message. DueTrail keeps that beside the amount.

What you can do in this version:

- Add invoices with a reference, client, currency, total, due date, and amount already paid.
- See outstanding balances in each currency. USD, EUR, PKR, and the rest are never added into one total.
- Work a queue of missed promises, overdue invoices, and follow-ups you scheduled.
- Keep the buyer contact and a submission checklist on the invoice.
- Copy a friendly, direct, or final reminder and send it yourself. DueTrail does not email or message anyone.
- Record a partial payment, a dispute, or paid in full. Paid and disputed invoices leave the queue.
- Keep a manual activity history.
- Export CSV or a JSON backup. The file stays on your device.

Who it is for: freelancers and small studios in Pakistan invoicing overseas clients, and freelancers elsewhere who chase direct invoices the same way.

This version stores data only in the browser or on the device. There is no account and no sync. Browser and app storage is not encrypted, not a backup, and not audit-grade evidence. Use fictitious sample data. Do not put confidential client or bank details in the demo.

The free demo is what this build includes. Solo at US$9/month and Team at US$19/month are planned prices for a later version with accounts and sync. They are not for sale in this build. There is no checkout and no card form. A Pakistan-localized price still has to be validated.

DueTrail does not collect debts, calculate interest, give legal advice, or guarantee that a client will pay.

## Privacy text

Use this for the App Store privacy details, the Google Play Data safety form, and the in-app disclosure. It matches the demo.

DueTrail stores the invoices, notes, reminder templates, and activity you type on this device only. The web demo uses browser storage. The iOS and Android shells use the same on-device web storage inside the app. That storage is not a secure archive and not audit-grade evidence.

This build does not create an account, does not sync to a server, and does not send email, WhatsApp, or any other message. Copying a reminder only puts text on the clipboard. You send it yourself.

This build does not collect payment card numbers, bank details, location, contacts from the phone, or analytics. There is no advertising identifier. Planned Solo and Team prices are not a live purchase.

You can remove the data by clearing the site data in the browser, deleting the installed home-screen app, or uninstalling the store app. A JSON backup is a file you download yourself. DueTrail does not upload it.

Answers to expect on the store questionnaires for this build:

- Data collected: none transmitted off the device.
- Data stored on device: invoices and notes the user types, including any names or email addresses they choose to enter.
- Tracking: no.
- Account required: no.
- Payments in the app: no. Do not enable Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing until a real account product exists.

If a later version adds accounts, sync, or a subscription, rewrite this text and the store forms before that version ships.
